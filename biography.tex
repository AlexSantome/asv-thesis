\chapter*{Biographical Sketch}
\pdfbookmark[0]{Biographical Sketch}{biosketch}

Alejandro Santomé Valverde obtained a B.Sc. degree in telecommunications engineering from the Universidade de Vigo, Spain, in 2019. 
During his bachelor period he did an internship in Attlantic Research Center, developing 5G small cell station for the Antenna Systems group.
Furthermore, last period of these studies were undertaken at the Hochschule für Technik und Wirtschaft des Saarlandes, Germany, were he developed his
bachelor thesis being part of RI-ComET research group.

Then, he completed a M.Sc. degree in telecommunication technologies, systems and networks from the Universitat Politècnica de Valencia, Spain, together with
a M.Sc. degree in telecommunications engineering. Both master's theses were developed in the Photonic Integrated Devices research group of Nanophotonics Technology Center.

In 2021 he joined iPronics as a photonics engineer in the photonic integrated circuits departmen, where he led the designs of two european projects. 
Then, he moved to software department where he is currently working at the Core-Software group.
